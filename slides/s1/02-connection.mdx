<Note appearAt={1} hideAt={2}>

**Nothing runs your query yet.** The postmaster accepts the connection,
authenticates it, and then does no work on it at all.

</Note>

<Note appearAt={2} hideAt={3}>

**It forks.** One connection becomes one process, for the life of that
connection. Your session's `SET`s, its prepared statements, its temporary
tables and its `work_mem` allocations all live inside that process and die
with it.

</Note>

<Note appearAt={3} hideAt={4}>

**Only the bottom half is shared.** Page cache, the lock table and WAL are
one copy for the whole server. Everything above them is per-process, which is
why a connection is not free and why `max_connections` is a number with
consequences.

</Note>

<Note appearAt={4}>

**Two panes, two processes.** Every slide in this series runs `s1` and `s2` on
separate connections, so these are two real backends — which is the whole of
sessions 4 and 5.

</Note>
