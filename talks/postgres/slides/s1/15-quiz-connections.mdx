export const title = "Quiz: connections";

# Your app holds 50 open connections. How many backend processes serve them?

<Choices revealAt={2}>
  <Choice>One: the postmaster handles all of them</Choice>
  <Choice>One per CPU core, shared between connections</Choice>
  <Choice correct>Fifty, one per connection, idle or not</Choice>
  <Choice>None until a query arrives</Choice>
</Choices>

<Note appearAt={2}>

**One connection, one process, for its whole life.** The postmaster only
forks. An idle connection still has its process, with its caches, `SET`s and
temporary tables, which is why `max_connections` is not free.

</Note>
