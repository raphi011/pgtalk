# The executor pulls

Every node answers one question: *give me your next row*.

<Diagram cols={12} rows={5}>
  <Box id="agg"  x={4.5} y={0} w={3} label="Aggregate" sub="count(*)" appearAt={1} />
  <Box id="filt" x={4.5} y={1.8} w={3} label="Filter" sub="status = 'shipped'" appearAt={1} />
  <Box id="scan" x={4.5} y={3.6} w={3} label="Seq Scan" sub="orders" appearAt={1} />

  <Arrow from="agg" to="filt" label="next?" appearAt={2} pulse="back" pulseAt={5} />
  <Arrow from="filt" to="scan" label="next?" appearAt={3} pulse="back" pulseAt={4} />

  <Highlight target="agg" appearAt={7} />
</Diagram>

<Note appearAt={1} hideAt={2}>

**`count(*)` over the shipped orders, as a tree.** Nothing runs until the
root is asked for a row.

</Note>

<Note appearAt={2} hideAt={4}>

**Requests go down.** `Aggregate` needs input, so it asks `Filter` for a row,
and `Filter` asks `Seq Scan`.

</Note>

<Note appearAt={4} hideAt={5}>

**One row comes back up.** The scan reads the next tuple from its current page
and returns it. Just the one.

</Note>

<Note appearAt={5} hideAt={6}>

**One row, or skip.** `Filter` tests `status = 'shipped'`. A row that fails is
never returned: the filter asks the scan again. A row that passes goes up, and
`Aggregate` adds one.

</Note>

<Note appearAt={6} hideAt={7}>

**Repeat 500,000 times**, then `Aggregate` returns its single row. Rows move
one at a time; nothing between two nodes is ever a full intermediate result.

</Note>

<Note appearAt={7}>

**So a node can stop asking.** Put a `LIMIT 10` where `Aggregate` is and the
scan stops after ten matching rows. It is never asked for the eleventh. Under
`count(*)` it cannot stop, because an aggregate needs every row before it can
answer.

</Note>
