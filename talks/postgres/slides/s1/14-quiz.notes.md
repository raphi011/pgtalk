## What this slide is for

It announces the quiz (A3d) and sets the rules before the first question, so
the room knows to wait for the options and commit before the reveal.

## Running it

Say how it works once, here, rather than on every question: the options
appear, you ask for hands on each letter, then step to the answer. Keep it
light. The point is to find out which idea did not land, not to score anyone,
and a room that fears being wrong keeps its hands down.
